RESTRICTED LAUNCH SCOPE

Low-risk digital work first

For an initial commercial pilot, use only legally reviewed services such as writing, editing and digital content preparation that do not require privileged account access, unsafe site visits, professional licenses or handling of vulnerable people.

Do not accept hazardous repair, transport, childcare, medical services, deceptive marketing, fake reviews, payment-to-work demands, or sensitive credentials. Keyword screening is imperfect: all genuine requests still need trained human review.
