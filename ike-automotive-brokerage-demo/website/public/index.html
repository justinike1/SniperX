Questions

Do you sell the vehicle directly?

No. The licensed dealer sells the vehicle. IKE supports planning, comparisons, and coordination within the agreed scope.

Are terms final in this preview?

No. Some service terms are still under internal review and remain configurable until approved.

Will this submit credit applications?

No. This application does not submit credit, process payments, or execute dealer transactions.

Can clients track progress privately?

Yes. Cases can receive time-limited private progress links with scoped access and revocation.
