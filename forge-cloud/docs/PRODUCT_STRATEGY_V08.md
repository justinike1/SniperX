# Forge: one ambition, disciplined launch

## Vision
Forge can eventually become a global, trusted system for economically productive work across many industries, but its first commercial product must solve a narrow demand-fulfillment problem.

## Product separation
1. **Forge Missions:** short, buyer-defined paid assignments. In v0.8 these are 100% simulated and cannot pay or dispatch. First launch candidates: permitted non-sensitive remote digital work.
2. **Forge Careers:** externally sourced jobs from permitted employer feeds. These are employment advertisements, NOT Forge assignments. Applicants apply with the original employer.
3. **Forge Work Universe:** navigable industry taxonomy to help the user discover professions, not a claim of open positions or qualified employment.
4. **Forge Trust:** verification workflow tracking today; authoritative provider connections and jurisdiction-specific human reviews before regulated dispatch.

## First paid experiment (only after release gates)
- Interview at least 10 prospective buyers, not only workers.
- Get 3–5 real businesses to request a narrow service with clear inputs and deliverables.
- Confirm price, delivery time, revision terms and margin BEFORE claiming traction.
- Obtain payments, contract, insurance, worker-classification and privacy/legal reviews appropriate to the activity.
- Manually coordinate initial eligible assignments; measure successful acceptance, quality, complaints, refunds and repeat orders.
- Only scale categories with repeat demand and positive fully loaded contribution margin.

## North-star measure
Weekly **accepted customer-funded work value**, broken down by real/simulated source. Do not combine test GMV with real revenue or worker earnings.

Support metrics: first-paid-assignment conversion, verified buyer repeat rate, on-time completion rate, dispute rate, gross margin after refunds and payment processing, provider costs, trust incidents and customer/worker satisfaction.

## Launch gates
No real operations, payments, globally marketed coverage or regulated direct assignments until applicable licenses, privacy law, worker classification, background-check consent, payment infrastructure, safeguarding, insurance and security reviews have been addressed per jurisdiction. This is not legal advice.

## Current implementation
- Implemented: private accounts, demo mission lifecycle, sourced external job connectors when configured, review tracking, service template catalog, taxonomy and UX.
- Missing: real customers, commercial agreements, real screening, payment rails, compliant jurisdictional service rules, managed horizontal scaling, automated retryable sync, independent security audit.
