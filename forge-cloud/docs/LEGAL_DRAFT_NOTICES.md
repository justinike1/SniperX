# PRELIMINARY CUSTOMER & PROVIDER NOTICES — NOT READY TO PUBLISH
**Draft only. Must be reviewed and rewritten as necessary by NJ counsel.** These are product-messaging examples, not binding Terms or Privacy Policy.

## Customer checkout notice — example
“You are requesting a defined professional service from a provider through Forge. The agreed work, deadline, review process, total price and cancellation rules are shown before you confirm. Forge does not guarantee a provider will be available. Payments may be collected by our designated payment provider. Before any live transaction we will disclose the applicable refund and dispute policy, platform role and fees.”

## Worker assignment notice — example
“This is an invitation to express interest in a specific service project, not a guarantee of a job, employment or earnings. Before you accept an approved project, Forge will show the scope, the customer’s acceptance rules, the amount allocated to you, potential deductions or fees and known timing restrictions. You can decline projects. Your legal status as an employee or independent contractor depends on the actual working relationship and governing law, not the way this page is titled.”

## Worker funds notice — example
“Amounts shown as pending are not available for withdrawal. Actual withdrawal eligibility and time to your bank depend on completed funding, provider checks, available balance, payment method and processing network. We will display any optional faster-payout fee and estimated net amount before confirmation. A faster payout may be unavailable and is never guaranteed.”

## Privacy notice — facts counsel needs verified first
- Entity legal name/address/contact, controller/processor roles, Stripe and ID-provider disclosures.
- Precisely which personal information Forge collects: profile, identity verification outcomes (not raw identity numbers), service preferences, task documents, usage/auth data, approximate location (if used), payment identifiers, support tickets and fraud records.
- Legal basis and purpose, sharing with buyers/providers/Stripe, data minimization, data retention, user rights, privacy request process and cookies. Explicitly explain whether workplace location and sensitive information are processed.
- Whether consumer privacy laws apply including NJ Data Privacy Act and other jurisdictions; disclosures and opt-outs for processing where required.
- Incident notifications, access and deletion limitations, handling payment and dispute records, children/minors restriction, data security expectations.

## Prohibited marketing lines without evidence and legal review
- “Guaranteed money today.” / “Instant pay for everybody.”
- “Job approved by the government.” / “FDIC-insured Forge wallet.” / “Protected escrow.”
- “All workers are verified.” / “Everyone is legally an independent contractor.”
- “Your bill can always be paid tomorrow.”
- “Eliminates homelessness” without validated causal evidence.

## Supporting company policies counsel must prepare
Terms of service; buyer agreement; provider agreement; order acceptance and change-order policy; cancellation/refund/dispute terms; platform fee disclosures; electronic consent; privacy notice; accessibility policy; worker complaints/anti-discrimination; marketplace restricted activities and safety policy; staffing/W-2 partner agreement if offered later; vendor/processor DPA; incident notice processes.
