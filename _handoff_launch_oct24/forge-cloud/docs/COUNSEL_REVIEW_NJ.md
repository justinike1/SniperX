# SNIPERX FORGE — NEW JERSEY COUNSEL REVIEW PACKET
**Preliminary issue spotter, NOT attorney approval or legal opinion.** Requested deliverable: a written signed applicability memo and approved legal documents before real users/money.

## Facts for counsel to validate (not assumed resolved)
NJ-based founder proposes an online two-sided marketplace, initially limited to adults in NJ buying/selling short professional-service assignments. Buyers post jobs and pay via Stripe Connect; a platform may set fee and release contractor proceeds after buyer acceptance and dispute period. Platform displays sourced traditional jobs separately and contemplates W-2 shift enquiries (not payroll). Users choose which projects to accept; some projects may be performed on customer premises in later phases. Provider classification depends on actual facts, not labels. Work is facilitated by an app, with screening/feedback/quality controls, potentially price guidance and dispute authority. The entity's final legal name, customer terms, insurance and payment contracts remain to be verified.

## Written legal decisions requested
1. **Employment status:** Apply NJ ABC test (including 2026 operative regulatory guidance) to each catalog category and the actual degree of platform/buyer control over price, time, place, tools, acceptance, subcontracting, exclusivity and relationship duration. Document if work must be W-2 or other legally protected category. Identify employer of record for every W-2 offering.
2. **Placement regulation:** Determine if Forge must register or license as NJ **job listing service, employment agency, consulting/temp agency or temporary help service firm**. Separate direct contracting from placing personnel. Confirm whether temporary labor provisions, designated classifications and bonding apply. No W-2 shifts live until this review is complete.
3. **Payment law:** Determine merchant of record and liability, worker/buyer contract, Stripe Connect suitability, payout arrangements, any money transmitter or escrow/licensing concerns, abandoned funds, gift cards/balance representation, chargebacks, reserves and payouts; specifically approve product wording without using 'escrow' or 'guaranteed instant payout' incorrectly.
4. **Tax:** Determine applicable federal/state information returns, marketplace withholding/backup withholding, 1099 reporting, sales tax on services, NJ employer tax obligations if workers are employees, and record retention. Consult tax CPA separately.
5. **Consumer protection:** Approve truthful job descriptions, seller verification claims, refund/acceptance policy, platform fee, no hidden worker signup fee, deceptive placement prohibition, claims about economic or homelessness impact, complaints and FTC advertising rules.
6. **Privacy:** Identify NJ Data Privacy Act, state breach notifications, biometric/identity vendor agreements, CCPA or other state reach, children/minors exclusion, data retention/deletion, cookie/session policies, location processing and sensitive data controls.
7. **Background screening:** If used, check FCRA, consent, adverse action and state/local anti-discrimination rules. Do not infer 'background checked' from worker self-attestation.
8. **Licenses & insurance:** General/professional liability, cyber and E&O; workers' compensation if required; occupational licensing for physical/regulated categories; commercial auto, household work, childcare, hazardous tasks out of initial scope.
9. **Contract documents:** Produce attorney-approved marketplace Terms, service provider agreement, buyer agreement/order terms, marketplace refund/claims policy, Privacy Notice, consent records, IP assignment for developers, and dispute jurisdiction/arbitration decisions as appropriate.
10. **Accessibility and claims:** Accessible worker/customer experience, English/Spanish translation review, disability accommodation and equal opportunity protections. Vet platform's advertised 'earn today' statements for accurate evidence.

## Questions counsel must explicitly answer in a signed memo
- Are **independent professional projects** in the defined pilot actually outside employer-employee relationships under NJ ABC law, under the exact workflow and platform controls shown in a screen recording?
- Is Forge a regulated employment/placement service by arranging these assignments or listing third-party jobs, even without staffing directly?
- Can Forge lawfully hold payment proceeds after charge before sending them to providers through separate charges and transfers, and under what contractual limits? How should funds and platform role be described?
- What registrations and insurance must be active before the first customer or provider joins? Does remote work by users in other states trigger additional obligations?
- Can Forge charge an optional express withdrawal fee to contractor proceeds? What disclosures/consents are needed? Is there any restriction related to any employee compensation? 
- What policy is legally enforceable for cancellations, non-delivery, time limits, revisions, buyer acceptance and disputes?
